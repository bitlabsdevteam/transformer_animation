How to use

Type any text and press Run. GPT-2 (small) runs a forward pass, then a backward pass of the next-token loss. Every intermediate tensor is drawn as a 3D bar field. Rows are tokens, columns are dimension bins, and bar height and colour show the value.

▶ Forward animates data flowing embedding → 12 blocks → logits. ◀ Backward animates the gradient ∂L/∂x flowing back from the loss.

Click any bar to see the exact calculation with real numbers. The panel also re-derives the gradient by hand and checks it against PyTorch autograd.
